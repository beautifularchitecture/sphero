### Summary

The performance and security targets follow Q1–Q7 faithfully, every artifact has its Assumptions section, and the unit's kind was respected when choosing which artifacts to produce. The blocking problems are in what was left undefined rather than what was written: the connection indicator and stale marker have no lifecycle beyond a dropped stream, the partial-telemetry rule contradicts BR1.5.3 on null values, Contract 2's delivery-guarantee open question is never taken up, and the security posture omits the unauthenticated command path that can move the robot.
